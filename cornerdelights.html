Corner Delights

I worked with a small convenience stand in Guelph to create a warm and inviting brand. The stand is only open during summer. I created the branding by taking inspiration by the summertime and the 'delights' part of the name. Forming the smile-sun logo and pairing it with a retro inspired typeface.

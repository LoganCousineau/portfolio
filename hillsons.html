Hill & Sons

This design is targeted towards young adults that are looking for a less alcoholic, and more fun drink. The design mimics the fun of a party, as well as taking inspiration from notebook doodles.

The illustrations were all hand-drawn by me. The goal of a package is to end up in the hands of a consumer. Having a bunch of small illustrations on the package encourages a potential customer to pick up the drink to inspect and look at the details.
